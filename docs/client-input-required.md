# Client input required before public content approval

Mark an item complete only after the named MBA owner supplies and approves the source. Leave unknown values blank. Do not infer claims, dates, rights, or personal information from sample content.

## Brand and company

- [ ] Approved logo, favicon, brand colors, typefaces, and usage rules
- [ ] Exact legal company name, registration details, and approved public address
- [ ] Public phone, WhatsApp number, email, opening hours, and emergency/holiday wording
- [ ] Confirmed intervention zones and the precise wording MBA approves for them
- [ ] Approved company history, team names/roles/photos, and biographies
- [ ] Social profile URLs and account owner
- [ ] Quote recipient mailbox, service expectations, and any approved response-time promise
- [ ] Approved map pin/address and embed consent/configuration

## Each product (`mba_product`)

- [ ] Approved product name, URL slug, excerpt, and full description
- [ ] Product category and application types
- [ ] Verified materials/profile information, configurations, glazing options, finishes/colors, and maintenance guidance
- [ ] Benefits/applications phrased and checked by the responsible MBA product owner
- [ ] Any technical performance values paired with a dated manufacturer/source document and exact units
- [ ] Warranty, certification, compatibility, availability, lead-time, and price statements individually approved or omitted
- [ ] Cover image, ordered gallery, and any color swatches; source filename, image owner, usage permission, credit, caption, alt text, and crop/focal-point guidance for each
- [ ] Related FAQs and projects checked for accuracy
- [ ] Technical PDF reviewed for current revision, rights, accessible text, and public sharing permission
- [ ] Product owner and approval date

## Each project (`mba_project`)

- [ ] Written client permission for the project story and each identifiable person/property
- [ ] Approved title, summary, challenge, solution, and result; support every outcome claim with client-approved facts
- [ ] Exact completion date (separate from publication date), public city/region, and confirmation that finer location is safe to disclose
- [ ] Installed products verified by the project owner and linked to the correct catalogue entries
- [ ] Before/during/after gallery order and whether each image may be cropped or retouched
- [ ] Per-photo author/source, copyright/license, consent limits, caption, alt text, and privacy review (remove plates, faces, addresses, screens, and personal items unless explicitly cleared)
- [ ] Related testimonial reviewed separately for exact wording, attribution, rating, photo permission, consent record, and withdrawal contact
- [ ] Project owner and approval date

## Each FAQ (`mba_faq`)

- [ ] Customer question approved in natural French
- [ ] Answer verified by the responsible technical/commercial owner; include scope, conditions, and source date
- [ ] Related product references checked and selected in the editor
- [ ] No unsupported safety, warranty, regulatory, performance, or response-time claim
- [ ] FAQ owner and review date

## Media, rights, and operations

- [ ] Original, high-resolution files supplied through the approved channel; no competitor images, scraped copy, or unlicensed logos
- [ ] Consent/license covers the intended website, social channels, geography, duration, edits, and attribution
- [ ] Record who approved each asset, on what date, evidence location, expiration/withdrawal rules, and replacement contact
- [ ] Approved French copy and future translation scope
- [ ] Legal/privacy/cookie wording reviewed by the responsible professional
- [ ] Production SMTP, analytics, map, and deployment credentials supplied through the approved secrets channel, never in this checklist or Git
