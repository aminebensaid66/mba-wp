# MBA Menuiseries Belhaj Ali — Website Features & WordPress Specification

**Project type:** Corporate showcase and lead-generation website  
**Business:** MBA Menuiseries Belhaj Ali  
**Specialty:** Custom aluminium joinery, fabrication, and installation  
**Primary market:** Tunisia  
**Recommended primary language:** French  
**Reference reviewed:** [ALU SYSTEM Tunisia](https://www.alusystem.tn/)  
**Review date:** 13 September 2026

> This document uses the competitor only as a market reference. The new website should have its own identity, wording, photography, structure, and visual design. Product claims, certifications, warranties, service areas, and years of experience must be confirmed by MBA before publication.

## 1. Project Goals

The website should:

1. Present MBA as a professional and trustworthy aluminium specialist.
2. Show the quality of its work through strong project photography.
3. Explain its products clearly enough to help customers choose.
4. Generate qualified quote requests by form, phone, and WhatsApp.
5. Let the owner independently update photos, products, projects, company details, and articles.
6. Rank on Google for relevant local searches in Tunisia.
7. Work especially well on mobile, where many visitors will call or use WhatsApp.

## 2. Competitor Analysis

### What ALU SYSTEM currently offers

The competitor website includes:

- A detailed product catalogue covering aluminium windows, sliding systems, verandas, glass partitions, curtain walls, shutters, mosquito screens, guardrails, brise-soleil, and facade cladding.
- A homepage with a large visual introduction, company presentation, product highlights, partners, articles, and repeated quote calls to action.
- Separate pages for company presentation, products, projects, blog, FAQ, and contact.
- A project gallery organized around named installations and locations.
- A contact page containing telephone, email, address, working hours, showroom/atelier details, service zones, map, and a quote form.
- Trust signals such as history, partner brands, certifications, technical expertise, materials, and after-sales service.
- An educational blog targeting questions about prices, product selection, insulation, maintenance, renovation, and local service areas.
- Local SEO content and structured information for a Tunisian business.
- Social links and a downloadable brochure.

### Strengths to learn from

- Broad coverage of search terms and customer questions.
- Strong emphasis on made-to-measure products and installation.
- Many opportunities for visitors to request a quote.
- Project photos are used as proof of capability.
- Product benefits are explained in customer language: light, space, security, insulation, durability, and design.
- Company credentials and partner brands reduce perceived risk.

### Opportunities for MBA to do better

- Use a simpler navigation and avoid overwhelming visitors with too many equal-level menu items.
- Give every project its own page, story, product tags, and photo gallery instead of presenting only a long gallery.
- Create a shorter, smarter quote form with product selection and photo/plan upload.
- Add a persistent mobile WhatsApp and call action.
- Use consistent, high-quality original photography with useful captions.
- Separate products into understandable categories and make filtering easy.
- Make technical benefits visual and easy to compare.
- Keep the interface fast and avoid unnecessary animations or heavy plugins.
- Give the owner clean, purpose-built WordPress fields rather than asking them to edit complex page-builder layouts.
- Make trust elements prominent: process, warranty, response time, materials, workmanship, and genuine customer reviews.

## 5. Page-by-Page Features

### 5.1 Global header and navigation

- MBA logo linked to the homepage.
- Compact main navigation with a product dropdown or mega-menu.
- Prominent `Demander un devis` button.
- Click-to-call telephone number on desktop and mobile.
- Optional language switcher if Arabic or English is added later.
- Sticky header after the visitor begins scrolling.
- Accessible mobile menu.

### 5.2 Homepage

Recommended sections, in order:

1. **Hero:** Original photo or short optimized video of a flagship MBA installation; clear value proposition; `Demander un devis` and `Voir nos réalisations` actions.
2. **Quick trust bar:** Years of experience, made-to-measure fabrication, service area, installation/after-sales service, or verified certifications.
3. **Main solutions:** Six to eight product cards with image, short benefit, and product-page link.
4. **Why choose MBA:** Craftsmanship, quality materials, precision, customization, respect for deadlines, installation, and support—only where accurate.
5. **Featured projects:** Latest or manually selected projects with product and location labels.
6. **How a project works:** Contact → site visit and measurements → proposal/quotation → fabrication → installation → handover/support.
7. **Options and finishes:** Colors, glazing, profiles, handles, motorization, and other available choices.
8. **Customer types:** Residential, architectural, commercial, and professional projects.
9. **Testimonials:** Genuine reviews, ideally including first name/company, city, project type, and consented photo.
10. **Partners/certifications:** Logos linking to a short explanation of what each relationship means.
11. **Advice/articles:** Three recent practical articles.
12. **Final conversion block:** Quote CTA, phone, and WhatsApp.

### 5.3 Company page — `L'entreprise`

- MBA's history and the story of Belhaj Ali.
- Workshop/team photography.
- Values and working standards.
- Capabilities: advice, measurement, fabrication, installation, and after-sales service.
- Team or founder introduction where desired.
- Workshop equipment or production process, if this adds credibility.
- Verified certifications, approvals, partners, and brands.
- Geographic intervention area.
- Link to featured projects and quote request.

### 5.4 Products listing

- Product cards grouped into clear categories.
- Filter by category or use case if the catalogue is large.
- Consistent product photography.
- Short customer-focused description for each solution.
- Direct link to its detail page.
- Quote CTA that records which product interested the visitor.

### 5.5 Individual product page

Each product should be maintained as a WordPress content item with these fields:

- Product name and category.
- Cover image and image gallery.
- Short introduction.
- Main customer benefits.
- Available opening/configuration types.
- Materials and profile systems.
- Glazing options.
- Colors and finishes.
- Dimensions or technical limits, where publishable.
- Thermal, acoustic, weather, and security characteristics, using only verified values.
- Manual/motorized options where relevant.
- Maintenance advice.
- Recommended applications.
- Downloadable technical sheet or brochure, where available.
- Related completed projects.
- Related FAQ questions.
- Product-specific quote button.

### 5.6 Projects listing — `Réalisations`

- Visual masonry/grid gallery.
- Filters for product category, project type, and location.
- Optional residential/commercial distinction.
- Featured projects at the top.
- Every image should have a useful alt description and optimized file size.

### 5.7 Individual project page

- Project name.
- Location at city/region level; never publish a private customer's exact address.
- Completion date or year.
- Client/project type.
- Products and solutions installed.
- Cover image and before/during/after gallery.
- Challenge, MBA solution, and result.
- Materials, colors, glass, profiles, and special options used.
- Testimonial, when available.
- Links to relevant product pages.
- `Vous avez un projet similaire ?` quote CTA.
- Social sharing image and SEO fields.

### 5.8 Quote page — `Demander un devis`

The form should be short enough to complete on a phone but detailed enough to qualify the request.

**Recommended fields:**

- Name and surname.
- Phone number (required).
- Email (optional or required according to MBA's workflow).
- City/governorate.
- Customer type: individual, architect, company, property developer, other.
- Project type: new construction, renovation, replacement, professional project.
- Product(s) of interest, with multiple selection.
- Approximate dimensions or quantity, optional.
- Desired timeframe, optional.
- Message.
- Upload photos, plans, or measurements; restrict type and size.
- Preferred contact method: phone, WhatsApp, email.
- Privacy consent checkbox.

**After submission:**

- Show a clear confirmation and expected response time.
- Email the request to the appropriate MBA address.
- Save the lead securely in WordPress or an approved CRM.
- Send an automatic acknowledgement to the customer.
- Include source/product data so MBA knows which page generated the enquiry.
- Protect the form against spam without adding frustrating puzzles.

### 5.9 Contact page

- Telephone, WhatsApp, email, and address.
- Opening hours.
- Clickable Google Maps location and directions link.
- Short contact/quote form.
- Service zones.
- Workshop/showroom visit information.
- Social media links.
- Emergency or after-sales contact only if MBA offers it.

### 5.10 FAQ

Organize editable questions into categories:

- Choosing a product.
- Aluminium versus PVC/wood.
- Glazing and insulation.
- Colors and finishes.
- Prices and quotation process.
- Measurement, fabrication, and installation timing.
- Cleaning and maintenance.
- Warranty and after-sales service.
- Intervention zones.

Relevant questions should also appear automatically on product pages. Add FAQ structured data only when the visible page and current Google rules make it appropriate.

### 5.11 Advice / Blog

The blog should answer genuine buying and maintenance questions rather than publish generic company news only.

Suggested initial topics:

- Fenêtre battante ou coulissante : comment choisir ?
- Quel vitrage choisir pour la chaleur et le bruit en Tunisie ?
- Comment entretenir une menuiserie aluminium ?
- Les facteurs qui influencent le prix d'une fenêtre aluminium.
- Volets roulants manuels ou motorisés.
- Comment préparer une demande de devis précise.
- Aluminium pour une villa en bord de mer: points de vigilance.
- Rénovation: quand faut-il remplacer ses fenêtres ?

Every article should include author/reviewer, publication and update dates, related products, related projects, and a contextual quote CTA.

### 5.12 Footer

- Short MBA description.
- Main page and product links.
- Contact details and opening hours.
- Service area.
- Social links.
- Legal notice and privacy policy.
- Cookie settings link where consent is required.
- Copyright year updated automatically.

## 6. WordPress Editing Experience

The owner's ability to update content is a core requirement. The site should use reusable templates and structured fields.

### Editable content types

| Content type | Owner can manage |
|---|---|
| Products | Title, category, descriptions, benefits, options, specifications, documents, cover, gallery |
| Projects | Title, location, date, project type, products used, story, specifications, gallery, testimonial |
| Testimonials | Customer label, location, project, review, rating, photo, visibility |
| FAQs | Question, answer, category, related products |
| Articles | Title, content, author, cover, categories, related products/projects |
| Partners | Name, logo, URL, description, display order |
| Global details | Phone, WhatsApp, email, address, hours, service zones, social links |

### Administration requirements

- Use the native block editor for articles and normal page content.
- Use structured custom fields for products and projects.
- Provide image guidance inside the admin: recommended ratio, minimum dimensions, and file size.
- Automatically generate responsive image sizes and WebP/AVIF versions where the server supports them.
- Allow projects and products to be reordered or marked as featured.
- Keep phone, address, hours, and social links in one global settings screen so they update everywhere.
- Give the owner an Editor-level account; reserve Administrator access for maintenance.
- Avoid editing critical layouts through free-form page-builder controls.
- Include a short PDF or screen-recorded owner guide and one handover/training session.

## 7. Conversion Features

### Must have

- Persistent WhatsApp button with a pre-filled but editable message.
- Click-to-call buttons on mobile.
- Quote CTA in the header, product pages, project pages, and footer.
- Product-aware quote form.
- Photo/plan upload.
- Clear submission confirmation and expected reply time.
- Genuine testimonials and completed-project evidence.

### Optional phase two

- Multi-step quote estimator—not an instant final price unless MBA has reliable pricing rules.
- Appointment booking for showroom or site visits.
- CRM integration and lead-status tracking.
- Automated WhatsApp Business workflow using an approved provider.
- Downloadable catalogue/brochure with lead capture.

## 8. Visual and Content Direction

- Position the brand as precise, modern, reliable, and technically competent.
- Use a restrained palette inspired by aluminium and architecture, then adapt it to MBA's existing logo.
- Give original project photography priority over stock imagery.
- Use spacious layouts, large details of finishes, and simple product diagrams.
- Avoid copying the competitor's wording, layouts, images, colors, or brand claims.
- Write concise French copy first; prepare the architecture for Arabic and English if they may be added.
- Maintain consistent image treatment and aspect ratios across the catalogue.
- Show technical terms alongside their practical customer benefit.

## 9. SEO and Local Discovery

- Unique page title, meta description, H1, URL, and copy for every product and major service location.
- Target natural terms such as `menuiserie aluminium Tunisie`, plus products and MBA's real service areas.
- Create service-area pages only when each contains useful, unique information and relevant local projects.
- Optimize Google Business Profile and keep name, address, phone, hours, and map details consistent.
- Add Organization/LocalBusiness, Breadcrumb, Article, Product/Service, and other valid structured data where appropriate.
- Add image alt text based on what the image actually shows.
- Generate XML sitemap, robots controls, canonical URLs, and redirects.
- Connect Google Search Console and a privacy-conscious analytics setup.
- Use internal links between products, projects, FAQs, and advice articles.
- Do not publish invented specifications, locations, reviews, or keyword-stuffed text.

## 10. Performance, Accessibility, Security, and Compliance

### Performance

- Mobile-first responsive design.
- Optimize and lazy-load below-the-fold images.
- Use properly sized responsive images rather than uploading full-resolution originals everywhere.
- Minimize plugin count, scripts, animation, and third-party trackers.
- Configure page caching, compression, and a CDN if appropriate.
- Aim for good Core Web Vitals on representative mobile devices.

### Accessibility

- Keyboard-operable navigation, forms, galleries, and dialogs.
- Visible focus states and sufficient color contrast.
- Correct heading hierarchy and form labels.
- Descriptive alternative text for informative images.
- Large touch targets and readable mobile typography.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Security and maintenance

- HTTPS everywhere.
- Automated daily database backups and regular full-site backups stored off-server.
- WordPress core, theme, and plugin update process with staging/rollback.
- Strong passwords, two-factor authentication for administrators, login-rate protection, and least-privilege roles.
- Spam and malicious-upload protection on forms.
- SMTP or transactional email configuration with delivery logging.
- Security monitoring and uptime monitoring.
- Remove unused themes/plugins and disable unnecessary public functionality.

### Privacy and legal

- Privacy policy explaining form, analytics, WhatsApp, map, and uploaded-file processing.
- Legal notice with accurate business information.
- Consent management for non-essential cookies and tracking.
- Retention and deletion policy for enquiries and customer-uploaded documents.
- Explicit permission for testimonials and project photos, especially at private homes.

## 11. Recommended Technical Approach

- Current supported WordPress and PHP versions at launch.
- A lightweight custom theme or carefully chosen block theme.
- Native Gutenberg blocks/patterns for normal content.
- Structured custom post types and fields for Products, Projects, FAQs, Testimonials, and Partners.
- A reliable form solution supporting file upload, spam controls, notifications, saved entries, and privacy consent.
- An SEO plugin, caching/performance layer, backup solution, security controls, and SMTP integration.
- Staging environment for updates and acceptance testing.
- Plugins should be selected during implementation based on licensing, hosting compatibility, maintenance history, and actual need; avoid locking the site into a large page-builder ecosystem without a clear reason.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria

The launch is complete when:

- The owner can add a product or project without developer assistance.
- Updating a global phone number or business hour changes it everywhere relevant.
- All forms deliver successfully, save the enquiry appropriately, and send confirmation.
- Uploaded files are restricted, protected, and included in the agreed lead workflow.
- Phone and WhatsApp actions work on mobile.
- Product/project filters and related-content links work correctly.
- Key pages are tested on current Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge at common desktop and mobile widths.
- Navigation and all core forms are usable by keyboard.
- No broken links, placeholder copy, unlicensed images, or invented claims remain.
- Search indexing, sitemap, analytics, backups, SMTP, security, and consent settings are verified.
- Performance is tested on representative pages, especially image-heavy project galleries.
- The owner receives credentials, documentation, and training.

## 15. Suggested Success Metrics

- Qualified quote submissions per month.
- Click-to-call and WhatsApp interactions.
- Conversion rate by product and project page.
- Organic visits from relevant Tunisian searches.
- Pages and search terms generating enquiries.
- Form delivery success and spam rate.
- Mobile performance and Core Web Vitals.
- Number of fresh projects/articles published by MBA after handover.

## Final Recommendation

MBA should build a focused lead-generation website rather than a simple online brochure. The strongest differentiator will be a structured portfolio of real work connected to clear product information and a frictionless quote process. WordPress should function as a safe content-management system for the owner—not as a collection of fragile page-builder layouts. Launch with excellent French content and original photography, then expand into multilingual content and advanced lead management only after the core website is producing reliable enquiries.
